Publication layout

  public/                 Ready-to-use game art, sound and fonts.
  assets/source/          Editable PNG atlases and stage artwork.
  assets/references/      Only selected generated artwork and CC0 sound originals.
  docs/                   Research links, prompts, audits and game captures.

A normal clone contains everything needed to install, build and play.
No Google Drive download or Git LFS setup is required for this publication.
Optional large, redistributable asset packs belong in versioned GitHub Releases.

Downloaded research photos, interview recordings, copied reference thumbnails
and retired asset archives are excluded from the public repository and its
initial published history. Their source links remain in the research reports.
Keeping a research citation does not relicense the referenced third-party work.
Historical preview pages may refer to these omitted local files.

Private creator accounts, sessions, uploaded photos and generated user storage
under data/ are excluded. .env files and API credentials are also excluded;
.env.example contains configuration names without credentials.
