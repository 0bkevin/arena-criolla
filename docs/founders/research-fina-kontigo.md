# Founder reference research: Fina and Kontigo

Checked 17 October 2026. This dossier verifies the requested CEO identities, records visual observations from downloaded photographs, and preserves photo provenance for later character art. The photographs were opened and inspected individually with `view_image` after download.

## Confirmed identities

**Fina: Enrique Rivas.** Fina’s current company history page identifies Enrique Rivas as CEO and places him among the four original partners. His public LinkedIn profile identifies his Fina role as co-founder and CEO. These sources support using Enrique Rivas as the founder/CEO character. [Fina — Nuestra Historia](https://www.finapartner.com/nuestra-historia/), [Enrique Rivas — public LinkedIn profile](https://ve.linkedin.com/in/enriquerivasz/en).

**Kontigo: Jesús Castillo.** Kontigo’s current Spanish team page labels Jesus Castillo “CEO & Co-founder”; its FAQ says Jesús Castillo and Gino Guatavita are the company’s co-founders. The company’s English whitepaper is authored by “Jesus Castillo, Co-founder & CEO.” Use **Jesús Castillo** as the display name; the company’s own pages omit the accent. [Kontigo — Quiénes somos](https://www.kontigo.com/quienes-somos), [Kontigo — English whitepaper](https://www.kontigo.com/en/whitepaper).

The assigned targets are not ambiguous after checking the first-party sources. Kontigo has other founders, but Jesús is the CEO requested here. “Jesús Castillo Ferrer” appears in a third-party radio transcript, but the company consistently uses “Jesus Castillo”; the shorter company-verified name is the safer canonical identity.

### Appearance and design cues

The named company portrait shows a young adult man with light-to-medium warm skin, a clean-shaven oval-to-oblong face, short dense black hair brushed up and slightly to the side, dark eyebrows and eyes, and no visible glasses. The event frames show the same clean-shaven appearance at a distance. No reliable evidence of facial hair or eyewear appears in the inspected materials. Confidence: high for clean-shaven/no-glasses and short dark hair; medium for precise face shape and skin tone because lighting varies.

For clothing, the current official portrait shows a plain black crew-neck T-shirt, dark blue jeans, and a dark wristwatch/fitness band. At the October 2024 pitch he wears a light polo, charcoal-gray zip jacket, blue jeans, sneakers, and an event lanyard. The official site and team portraits use Fina’s violet/purple identity with a magenta accent and white lettering; the official portrait also places him in a purple-branded office. Use violet with a restrained pink accent as company color, and the black tee as the strongest current outfit cue. [Fina — Nuestra Historia](https://www.finapartner.com/nuestra-historia/).

### Appearance and design cues

The current Kontigo portrait shows a young adult man with medium warm skin, a clean-shaven oval face with a rounded jaw, short black hair brushed back/up, dark eyes and brows, and no visible glasses. The interview images confirm the same general build and face. In the work-session frame he wears a dark cap and charcoal/black T-shirt; in the filmed-interview frame he wears a black collared polo and a vivid orange cap. The official portrait uses a navy V-neck T-shirt. Confidence: high for hair, clean-shaven face, no glasses, and medium skin; medium for the precise jaw/face proportions because of the varying cap and angle.

Kontigo’s current first-party site uses a near-black/navy interface with white text and a vivid orange accent (the rendered official page includes `#FF7400`). The interview orange cap echoes the accent, while the official team portrait offers a neutral navy outfit cue. Use navy/black clothing with orange as a small accent rather than assuming a full orange uniform. [Kontigo — Quiénes somos](https://www.kontigo.com/quienes-somos).
