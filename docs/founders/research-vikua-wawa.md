# Founder reference research: Vikua and La Wawa

Research checked on 17 October 2026. This note covers the current CEO evidence for Vikua and La Wawa, alongside the founder photo references. Role titles vary across dates and sources, so the evidence and date are recorded below rather than treating an older title as current.

**Scope clarification (17 October 2026):** the requested Wawa reference is its current CEO, not Mafe Vera. The current-CEO conclusion below therefore prioritizes newer 2026 role evidence and treats Mafe as a former CEO.

## Identity and current roles

**Juan José Pocaterra Mendoza is the best-supported current CEO reference for both Vikua and La Wawa.** For Vikua, the WEF profile currently calls him “Co-Founder and CEO of Vikua,” and VenAmCham's 2025 event post calls him “El CEO de Vikua.” The 2025 title is corroborated by the event agenda. For La Wawa, there is a title discrepancy: the WEF profile calls him “Co-Founder and President, Wawa,” while a recent Latam at Silicon Valley event bio identifies him as “CEO of wawa.” That event was part of Colombia Tech Week 2026 (10–16 August), and Juan's LinkedIn repost repeats the bio. Wawa's own recent post calls him a co-founder but does not specify his executive title. I therefore recommend using Juan for the current-CEO depiction across both companies, while recording that the freshest explicit CEO label for La Wawa is from the event organizer, not Wawa's company page, and WEF's profile uses President. ([WEF profile](https://www.weforum.org/people/juan-jose-pocaterra/), [VenAmCham's 2025 post](https://pe.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A7333237047857434624/), [VenAmCham 2025 agenda](https://www.venamcham.org/perspectivas-sociales-2025-invertir-en-las-personas/), [Latam at Silicon Valley event bio](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/latam-at-silicon-valley_latamatsiliconvalley-latam-siliconvalley-activity-7448436410983858176-8xPX), [Juan's repost of the event bio](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/juanpocaterra_a-full-week-of-latam-tech-conversations-in-activity-7450075472631205888-jzX_), [Colombia Tech Week 2026 dates](https://colombiatechweek.co/), [Wawa's recent co-founder post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lawawa_mobility-latamtech-culturalshift-activity-7448474913939689473-YByM)).

**María Fernanda “Mafe” Vera** is a La Wawa co-founder and former CEO, not the current-CEO reference for this task. Wawa called her CEO in its 2024 event post, and Juan described her as leading Wawa in a 2025 repost; her current public LinkedIn bio describes her as “Ex CEO y Co-Founder de La Wawa.” The newer 2026 event bio calling Juan Wawa CEO is the freshest explicit title evidence found. This is the Venezuelan shared-mobility startup at [enwawa.com](https://www.enwawa.com/), not the U.S. convenience-store chain. ([Mafe's current LinkedIn profile](https://es.linkedin.com/in/mar%C3%ADa-fernanda-vera-03a53031/en), [Wawa's 2024 post naming Mafe CEO](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lawawa_este-martes-4-de-junio-nuestra-ceo-mar%C3%ADa-activity-7201978294328635392-B-TF), [Juan's 2025 post crediting Mafe's leadership](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/juanpocaterra_as-both-a-husband-and-co-founder-im-incredibly-activity-7290406371252928512-Kicn), [2026 event bio naming Juan CEO](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/latam-at-silicon-valley_latamatsiliconvalley-latam-siliconvalley-activity-7448436410983858176-8xPX), [Bloomberg Línea interview](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/2022/06/13/mafe-vera-la-primera-mujer-al-frente-de-una-startup-de-tecnologia-en-venezuela/), [Wawa legal notice identifying the Venezuelan operator and Juan as authorized representative](https://www.enwawa.com/legal/terminos-y-condiciones-driver-app)).

The founder relationship is corroborated by Wawa's official public post sharing a Startups Latam profile that says the mobility startup was founded by Juan José Pocaterra Mendoza and María Fernanda Vera. The two founder identities are high confidence. Juan's Vikua CEO title is high confidence, with current WEF profile wording and a 2025 public event source. His current La Wawa CEO title is moderate confidence: a recent 2026 event biography explicitly calls him CEO, while WEF uses President and Wawa's post calls him co-founder without an executive title. Mafe's prior CEO role is well supported, but the new 2026 evidence points to Juan as the current CEO reference. ([Wawa post sharing the founder profile](https://es.linkedin.com/posts/lawawa_conoce-a-la-wawa-la-startup-de-transporte-activity-7087812637308137472-suXT), [WEF profile](https://www.weforum.org/people/juan-jose-pocaterra/), [VenAmCham's 2025 post](https://pe.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A7333237047857434624/), [2026 event bio](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/latam-at-silicon-valley_latamatsiliconvalley-latam-siliconvalley-activity-7448436410983858176-8xPX), [Wawa's recent co-founder post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lawawa_mobility-latamtech-culturalshift-activity-7448474913939689473-YByM)).

## Company visual cues

These are visual cues sampled from the companies' current public LinkedIn logos, not formal brand specifications. Vikua's logo uses a gray wordmark and an angular “V” mark with blue/teal, green, and orange facets on a white field. For character clothing, use restrained urban-tech smart casual: dark jacket or clean button-down, with one small accent in the mark's colors. ([Vikua company page/logo](https://www.linkedin.com/company/vikua/))

Wawa's current company logo tile is a rich purple field with a white wordmark. Use purple and white as the clearest company cues; the Wawa-branded white tee in Mafe's portrait supports a simple logo tee under a dark jacket. Juan's tan jacket and cap are specific panel clothing, not Wawa uniform. ([Wawa company page/logo](https://www.linkedin.com/company/lawawa/))
