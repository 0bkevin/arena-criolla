# Founder references: Carlos García Ottati and Victor Cardenas

Research checked 17 October 2026. This note verifies the two intended identities and records six inspected photo references for later character design. Image files are local visual references; their downloadability does not establish a license for redistribution or publication.

## Identity findings

**Carlos García Ottati — Kavak founder and CEO.** Kavak’s current company newsroom published a February 2026 message explicitly identified as being from CEO Carlos García Ottati. Kavak’s own history page describes its founders and identifies Carlos as the person whose used-car buying and selling experience led to the company. The company’s careers page currently labels him “Carlos García, Fundador y CEO.” These first-party sources agree on the identity and role. ([Kavak newsroom, Feb. 17, 2026](https://news-room.kavak.com/cerramos-2025-fuertes-empezamos-2026-aun-mas-fuertes), [Kavak history](https://www.kavak.com/mx/blog/historia-kavak), [Kavak careers](https://www.kavak.com/ar/empleos))

**Victor Cardenas Codriansky — Slash co-founder and CEO.** Slash’s current About page identifies Victor Cardenas as CEO. In Slash’s first-person April 2026 Series C announcement, Cardenas says he started Slash and identifies Kevin as his co-founder; Y Combinator’s Slash company profile lists Victor Cardenas as a founder and co-founder. Slash’s official LinkedIn identifies its CEO by the full professional name Victor Cardenas Codriansky. This resolves the requested identity as the Slash fintech executive; the similarly named Colombian DJ/producer at victorcardenas.com is a different person. ([Slash About](https://www.slash.com/company/about), [Slash Series C announcement, Apr. 16, 2026](https://www.slash.com/blog/series-c), [Y Combinator company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/slash), [Slash’s official LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/joinslash_people-have-asked-us-what-ai-first-banking-activity-7463284646466793472-nGam), [unrelated musician’s official site](https://victorcardenas.com/))

The interview page for a live LATAM at Silicon Valley recording calls Cardenas Venezuelan and identifies him as Slash’s founder. This supports the Venezuelan connection in the request, though that nationality statement comes from the interviewer’s event/interview page rather than Slash’s company biography. ([Startupeable interview, Apr. 19, 2026](https://www.startupeable.com/en/slash-victor-cardenas/))

## Appearance and wardrobe observations

### Carlos García Ottati

Across the three inspected references, Carlos has light to light-medium skin, an oval-to-long face, a straight nose, light brown hair, and no visible glasses or facial hair. Hair varies by date: the newer Kavak portrait shows short, brushed-up hair, while earlier editorial/event images show longer hair reaching the shoulders. A likeness should choose one dated look rather than blend the two. Clothing includes a dark blazer over a plain white crew-neck shirt in the Kavak portrait, a black Kavak zip jacket over a dark checked button-up in the workshop photo, and a black crew-neck shirt in the Forbes portrait. The clearest branded outfit cue is the black Kavak jacket with a white chest wordmark. The references favor black/dark navy and white, but do not establish a formal brand palette. These are observations from the photos, not a claim that he has a standard uniform.
