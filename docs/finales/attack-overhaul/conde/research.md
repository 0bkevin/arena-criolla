# Conde Bond research and design

Reviewed 2026-10-19. User-supplied visual reference: `../references/conde-conde-bond.png`. Canonical identity: `assets/source/conde-v3.png` (older broad face, gray goatee, stocky build). The supplied poster is the costume authority: white straw hat, black sunglasses, dark suit, open white shirt.

Primary distribution listing: [Apple TV, Er Conde Bond](https://tv.apple.com/pt/movie/er-conde-bond/umc.cmc.2gcgfeee0sz5kjqeqgw2egmag) identifies Benjamín Rausseo as Chuito and director and credits Conde Productions. [Globovisión's interview with Rausseo](https://www.dailymotion.com/video/xslilq) documents him promoting the movie in his own interview. No movie/video/audio is embedded or downloaded into the game.

Gameplay is fictional: self-transformation at super startup tick 9, normal 3-win/75-energy gates, 360 active gameplay ticks, 1.2 damage multiplier, no enemy capture or automatic damage. Normal combat remains available following the existing short super recovery. Stock loss clears the transformation; paying for a later activation refreshes its duration without multiplying the buff.
