## Suggested fictional game adaptation

These are design proposals, **not claims that he fights or performs these actions in real life**:

- Readable silhouette: forward black cap, large thick black glasses, visible throat rose and forearm tattoos, dark short-sleeved polo with light piping. Dark trousers and low-profile shoes can complete the game outfit as an art choice. Preserve normal adult body/head proportions.
- One-off welcome: lift a hand to adjust the glasses, open that hand toward the opponent in a presenter's greeting, then settle into guard. Selection can use a shorter single glasses adjustment; never loop the introduction.
- Theme: communication, open signal and information networks, grounded in the documented journalism/radio/digital-rights work. A fictional **“Señal abierta”** ability could send brief cyan radio-wave rings forward, with a visible wind-up and recovery for fair counterplay.
- A short broadcast-click/static cue, impact sounds and the game's existing generic nonverbal exertion/hurt sounds fit this theme. Do not add real speech clips, catchphrase imitations or a voice clone.

## Implemented art direction

The final artwork uses the backward cap and light-edged polo shown by Concordia, with Chiste Interno and VENtv used to cross-check facial proportions. The lower-body outfit completes the game silhouette as a design choice. The implemented introduction is a single nod and raised-guard acknowledgement (“Asiente y abre la señal”), followed by a settled stance; it does not claim the earlier proposed open-palm or glasses-adjustment action. The implemented special is named **Señal libre**. Original artwork, prompts, packing and independent review are in `docs/melanio-v44/`.
