Quality checks and format choices
  • All 218 source images tested as exact-quality AVIF. At the tested settings, only the social preview PNG became smaller. Converting the full image inventory to AVIF would increase it from 140.7 MB to 211.9 MB.
  • 40 published variants for 39 source images: smaller WebP files, one AVIF social preview and its PNG fallback. Practical threshold: at least 4 KiB and 1% saved. Compression saves 1.09 MB across the selected inventory; a player downloads only the assets their session needs.
  • Exact straight RGBA checks include RGB under fully transparent pixels, dimensions and color/orientation semantics. Native browser checks compare all pixels of every derivative. Originals are retained and automatically used if a derivative is missing, stale or corrupt.
  • Every-frame image provenance checked across 312 attack demos / 48,544 rendered frames, plus 3,300 complete 1080 × 640 RGBA comparisons. Target fixtures test rendering with substituted identities and mirrored capture snapshots; they do not replay combat physics for every opponent.
  • Audio, fonts, SVG and text stay byte-identical. Gzip/Brotli round trips were tested; production already has zstd/gzip through Caddy. Existing entrance AVIFs and the separately published offline guide remain unchanged.
  • The AVIF social preview decodes more slowly locally (110 ms versus 24 ms median) but saves 736 KB over its PNG source. It is outside gameplay. Local loopback loader times were mixed; the measured network saving comes from fewer bytes, not a claim of faster rendering.
