# Melanio movement and renderer review v45

Scope: adversarial review of the v44 movement claims against the actual atlas and `renderMatch` frame selection. The eight PNG atlas poses were inspected individually at combat scale and in the full transparent atlas. Source facing is right for every pose; the hurt frame leans left while keeping its gaze right. Run frames alternate contact legs and arm leads. Grounded poses have their lowest visible pixels on the cell baseline; jump frame 4 tucks both legs. The cap and glasses stay coherent in all eight unwarped combat frames. No clipping, floating grounded feet, wrong-side recoil, or identity stretching was found.

## Finding — signal special body pose did not follow its attack phases

**Severity: Medium (P2; resolved in this working tree). Confidence: High.** The v44 spec says: “Su preparación de 21 ticks a 60 Hz permite interrumpirlo; emite un único proyectil ondulante” ([review.md](../melanio-v44/review.md#personalidad-y-combate)). The special lasts 52 ticks total: 21 ticks of startup followed by 31 ticks of recovery. Before the v45 renderer branch, `renderMatch` selected block frame 1 only for attack ticks 0–3 and straight-punch frame 5 for every later `special` tick. A stationary Melanio therefore held the punch pose from ticks 4–51 (about 0.8 seconds), although the single pulse correctly launched at tick 21. This was a visual and combat-readability defect; hit timing, cost, and physics were correct. The focused real-engine regression reproduced frame 5 at tick 20; the phase expectation is guard at tick 20, punch at launch tick 21 through tick 28, then ready/run/jump according to actual recovery state. The signal-specific phase branch in [renderer.ts](../../src/game/renderer.ts#L184) now selects those poses while preserving stun priority.

`tests/melanio-render-v45.test.ts` runs `stepMatch` and the real `renderMatch` for both player sides. It checks run alternation, jump, block, light/heavy/super phases, the one-off intro/selection gesture through actual rig anchors, signal windup/release/recovery, airborne and moving recovery, and stun recoil. Focused verification: `npx tsx --test tests/melanio-render-v45.test.ts` — **3/3 passed** after the renderer correction.

Riskiest line: [renderer.ts](../../src/game/renderer.ts#L187), where signal-specific timing must remain aligned with `TECHNIQUES.signal.startup` if that timing changes.
